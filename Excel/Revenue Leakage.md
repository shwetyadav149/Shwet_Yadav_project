Project Title

Subscription Revenue Leakage & Customer Risk Analysis | Python & Machine Learning

Business Problem

Subscription-based businesses may generate less actual revenue than expected because of factors such as:

Excessive discounting
Customer refunds
Delayed payments
Billing or revenue collection issues
Customer behavior and engagement patterns

The business needs to understand where revenue is leaking, what factors are driving the loss, and which customers or transactions may require proactive intervention.

The objective of this project was to calculate revenue leakage, identify its key drivers through exploratory analysis, and build machine learning models to predict potential revenue leakage.
